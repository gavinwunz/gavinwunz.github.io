2026

VolFoundry

An arbitrage-checked implied volatility surface for crypto options. It pulls the live Deribit option chain, inverts for implied volatility, calibrates SVI, runs static-arbitrage checks, and fits an SSVI surface. The interesting part is not the fitting; it is deciding what to do when the market quotes something that cannot be arbitrage-free.

2026

FluxCap

A bottle cap that tells you whether the milk has gone off. Fresh milk sits near pH 6.7; as lactic acid builds it falls toward 4.6, and a halochromic ring reads that shift from green through yellow to red. No electronics, no app, nothing for anyone to remember to do. The CAD model is a two-shot injection-moulded HDPE housing with a sensor cartridge behind an ultrasonically welded ePTFE membrane. Costed to a $74,600 pilot; submitted to Velocity 2026.
