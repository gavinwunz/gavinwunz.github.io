Things I have built

VolFoundry

Quantitative research

An arbitrage-checked implied volatility surface for crypto options. It pulls the live Deribit option chain, inverts each quote for implied volatility, calibrates SVI slice by slice, runs static-arbitrage checks and fits an SSVI surface across the whole grid.

The fitting is the easy half. The interesting half is deciding what to do when the market quotes something that cannot be arbitrage-free. It does, most days, and a surface that just shrugs is worse than no surface.

FluxCap

Founder · Materials

A bottle cap that tells you whether the milk has gone off. Fresh milk sits near pH 6.7; as lactic acid builds it falls toward 4.6, and a halochromic ring reads that shift from green through yellow to red.

No electronics, no app, nothing for anyone to remember to do. The CAD model is a two-shot injection-moulded HDPE housing with a sensor cartridge behind an ultrasonically welded ePTFE membrane. Costed to a NZ$74,600 pilot.

JudgeGuard

Open source · Evaluation

Unit tests, regression tests and CI release gates for LLM-as-a-judge evaluators. Test the evaluator before you trust the evaluation.

Teams gate a release on an LLM judge and never test the judge itself. It can flip its verdict when you swap which answer is shown first, or disagree with itself on byte-identical input. JudgeGuard runs five deterministic probes against the evaluator and blocks the release on the first one that breaks.
